# Home surface

## Scope

Redesign the homepage and its connected catalog, coffee detail, and contact routes as one coherent public-facing site.

## Mode

Persuade

## Audience and action

The audience is inferred as people discovering and buying coffee from Oaxaca. Make origin and product details easy to scan, then let visitors ask about a selected coffee and presentation through the existing WhatsApp contact.

## Proof and constraints

Use the real Café Talante packaging, product photography, origin details, and existing contact routes. The listed prices come from `catalogo.txt` and may need confirmation; do not imply live inventory, shipping terms, certifications, or new producer-impact metrics. Keep the site static, responsive, accessible, and lightweight.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make the Oaxacan origin of each coffee feel like its own printed edition, while keeping the route to a real product inquiry immediate.

OWN-WORLD: One ink-dark print-shop world: charcoal paper, warm uncoated stock, restrained vermilion and agave accents, expressive editorial type, and assertive rules. The atlas supplies geographic wayfinding; teletext contributes a compact index, not its phosphor palette or fixed grid. Product detail remains literal and legible.

STORY: Visitors meet a real coffee, understand where it comes from and how it is processed, compare available presentations, and inquire through WhatsApp. The community story uses only existing copy and imagery.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A sticky masthead leads into an asymmetrical printed sheet. The real Sierra Norte package anchors the composition beside its origin, published 250 g price, size selector, and working WhatsApp inquiry action. A region index begins below the sheet.

FORM: Oaxacan cultural handbills and small print workshops, candidate 4 of 7; seed:14e740630d5ff247. Integrate the useful recommendations from all cards as information and interaction disciplines within this single visual world, never as competing skins.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
